About Us

Power and data,
under one roof.

Rain Transformer Engineering LLC is a boutique consulting firm that bridges two critical engineering disciplines: power transformer design for high-density data centers, and the data & AI engineering that drives modern infrastructure decisions.

Our founders bring complementary expertise — decades of high-voltage transformer design on one side, and production-scale data analytics, pipelines, and applied AI on the other. That rare combination means we can advise on the full stack, from the transformer vault to the analytics platform.
